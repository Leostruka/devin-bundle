## Key claims verified against the video

- **First sentence (Sutton):** "The biggest lesson that can be read from 70 years of AI research is that general methods that leverage computation are ultimately the most effective, and by a large margin."
- **Video quote (00:00:10.480–00:00:21.120):** matches Sutton's opening almost verbatim.
- **Sutton's nuance (paragraphs 1–2):** Researchers use human knowledge for short-term gains, but in the long run only leveraging computation matters; the two *need not* run counter, but in practice time spent on one is time not spent on the other.
- **Video paraphrase (00:00:57.920–00:01:07.280):** "that was probably just a waste of time ... nothing really matters in AI research apart from just improving the power of these machines."
- **Verification result:** The video paraphrase is more absolute than Sutton's text. Sutton does **not** say "nothing matters" or that optimization is always a waste of time; he says human-knowledge optimizations help in the short term but plateau and can inhibit further progress as computation scales. The video's closing is best treated as the presenter's interpretive gloss, not as the primary source's claim.
