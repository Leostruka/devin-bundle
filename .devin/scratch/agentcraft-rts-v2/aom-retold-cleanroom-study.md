# Age of Mythology: Retold - Clean-Room Architecture & Mechanics Study for RTSD

## 1. BANG! Engine & Visual Pipeline (Public Technical Analysis)

- **Engine Lineage**: *Age of Mythology: Retold* runs on World's Edge / Tantalus's modernized **BANG! Engine** (evolved from *Age of Empires III: Definitive Edition*).
- **Unit Collision Pushing & Weight Classes**:
  - Units apply soft radial separation forces when pathing or idling so squads form readable ranks instead of stacking on a single coordinate.
- **Visual Age & Tier Upgrades**:
  - Unit equipment (shields, blades, crests) and structure ornaments visually upgrade across the 5 Ages (`Archaic -> Classical -> Heroic -> Mythic -> Wonder`).
- **4K Skeuomorphic Pantheon HUD (Brian Flanagan UI Architecture)**:
  - Top bar integrates: **Population/Supply**, **Resource Stockpiles (Food, Wood, Gold, Favor)**, **Villager Priority**, **Circular Age Indicator (`I`..`V`)**, and the **Reusable God Powers Bar**.

## 2. Core Gameplay Systems Mapped to RTSD (`milestone/v3.8.0`)

| AoM: Retold System | Mechanics in AoM: Retold | Clean-Room Mapping in `RTSD` |
|---|---|---|
| **5 Ages (`I`..`V`)** | `Archaic (I) -> Classical (II) -> Heroic (III) -> Mythic (IV) -> Wonder (V)` | Derived from real session milestones (heroes deployed, quests completed, ledger gates passed) + manual Age-Up / Minor God patron choice via `POST /intent` (`order: "age_up"`). |
| **Divine Favor (`FAVOR`)** | 4th primary resource generated by Temples, Monuments, Combat, or Oracles; uncapped in Retold | Event-sourced `resources.favor` in `world.py`: earned from passed verification ledger gates (`+5` each), skill casts (`+3`), and completed quests (`+10`). |
| **Reusable God Powers (`#godbar`)** | Multi-use God Powers on cooldown with escalating Favor cost (`Vision`, `Restoration`, `Flaming Weapons`, `Lightning Storm / Meteor`) | Top-center `#godbar` with 4 real God Powers (`Vision`, `Restoration`, `Flaming Weapons`, `Lightning Storm`) that spend Favor, trigger 3D battlefield VFX + WebAudio DSP thunder/choir, and emit real `/intent` orders. |
| **Unit Pushing (Collision Separation)** | Units push neighbors aside when moving/stopping | Pairwise soft repulsion force in `stepMotion(dt)` keeping heroes/workers separated by `>= 18px` so squads fan out naturally around POIs and buildings. |
| **Villager Priority** | Auto-economy task ratio presets | Top-bar / Town Hall priority preset (`Balanced`, `Gold Focus`, `Quest Rush`, `Guard`) steering idle heroes/workers. |
